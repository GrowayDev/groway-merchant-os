## South African Specifics

### Currency Format
- Symbol: R (Rand)
- Format: R X XXX (space as thousands separator)
- Example: R 12 450,00

### Supported Networks
| Category | Providers |
|----------|-----------|
| Mobile | Vodacom, MTN, Cell C, Telkom, Rain |
| Banks | FNB, Capitec, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank, TymeBank, African Bank |
| Retailers | Shoprite, Checkers, Pick n Pay, Woolworths, Game, Makro, Dis-Chem |
| Food | KFC, Nando's, McDonald's, Steers, Debonairs |
| Transport | Gautrain, MyCiTi, Rea Vaya, Uber, Bolt |

### Regulatory Compliance
- **NCA**: National Credit Act (credit agreements)
- **NCR**: National Credit Regulator (credit provider registration)
- **FSCA**: Financial Sector Conduct Authority
- **POPIA**: Protection of Personal Information Act
- **CPA**: Consumer Protection Act (gift cards, laybye)
- **FICA**: Financial Intelligence Centre Act (KYC)
