# Design Notes

## Colour palette (brand-adjacent — AVOID blue/indigo)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg-slate-deep` | `#0B1220` | Full-bleed background for cold open, quote cards, comparison panels |
| `--bg-slate` | `#0F172A` | Default demo background |
| `--bg-surface` | `#1E293B` | Cards, panels, custody-chain nodes |
| `--emerald-primary` | `#10B981` | Primary accent — "AUTHENTIC", sidebar active, brand wordmark gradient stop |
| `--teal-secondary` | `#14B8A6` | Secondary accent — custody-chain line, gradient partner |
| `--amber-warm` | `#F59E0B` | Warm accent — used sparingly for "NEW" badge, timestamps, attention highlights |
| `--alert-red` | `#EF4444` | "COUNTERFEIT DETECTED", broken custody-chain link |
| `--text-primary` | `#F8FAFC` | Body text on dark |
| `--text-muted` | `#94A3B8` | Captions, attribution, subtext |

**Forbidden:** `#3B82F6`, `#6366F1`, `#8B5CF6`, and any blue/indigo/violet gradient. The palette must read "South African township at dusk" — warm-amber street light against cool slate, with emerald as the signal of trust.

## Typography

- **Serif** (cold-open, quote card, brand end-card wordmark): `Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond` — weights 500–700.
- **Sans-serif** (all UI captions, narration overlays, status badges): `Inter` or `Geist` — weights 400 for body, 600 for labels, 700 for status badges ("AUTHENTIC", "COUNTERFEIT DETECTED").
- **Mono** (timestamps, GTIN strings, "412 ms"): `JetBrains Mono` or `Geist Mono` — weight 500.

## Caption positions

- **Narration captions** (scenes 1–14): bottom-centre, 18% from bottom, max-width 70% of frame, 32–40 px sans-serif, semi-transparent dark-slate pill with 12 px padding, 8 px radius, 1 px emerald hairline border at 40% opacity.
- **Status badges** ("AUTHENTIC", "COUNTERFEIT DETECTED"): top-centre, 8% from top, 28 px bold, emerald or red pill.
- **Custody-chain labels**: appear directly below each node, 14 px mono uppercase, muted colour.
- **Source attribution** (Scene 2): top-right, 14 px sans-serif italic, muted slate.
- **End-card credit**: bottom-centre, 40 px from bottom, 18 px sans-serif medium, emerald underline that draws left-to-right over 600 ms.

## Transitions

- **Scene 1 → 2**: 400 ms crossfade, audio duck under piano note.
- **Scene 2 → 3**: hard cut on the word "weeks" — increase tension.
- **Scene 3 → 4**: 300 ms fade-up from black to the browser; first appearance of the app.
- **Vertical switches (Scene 7, 8, 9)**: 600 ms smooth crossfade with a subtle 4 px blur on the outgoing frame.
- **Scene 11 (AUTHENTIC)**: emerald glow pulse — 1.2 s ease-out — radiates from the green checkmark outward through the custody-chain nodes.
- **Scene 12 (COUNTERFEIT DETECTED)**: 150 ms red flash (full-frame 18% opacity), then settle. Custody-chain link breaks with a 300 ms dash animation.
- **Scene 13 → 14**: 500 ms crossfade into the emerald-to-teal gradient end-card.
- **End-card credit underline**: 600 ms left-to-right draw, 2 px emerald line.

## Motion principles

- **Slow, deliberate, sober** for Act 1 — no quick cuts, no kinetic type. Let the numbers land.
- **Confident, brisk** for Act 2 — clicks happen on the beat of the narration; cursor should move with intent, not wander.
- **Tight, evidentiary** for Act 3 — every UI element revealed should feel like evidence being presented (timestamps, badges, chain nodes).
- **Quiet, resolved** for Act 4 — let the credit sit for the full 2 seconds it deserves.

## Accessibility & safety

- All captions rendered with a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio against their background.
- The red "COUNTERFEIT DETECTED" flash is capped at 18% frame opacity and 150 ms duration — well below WCAG 2.3.1 photosensitive seizure threshold (no more than 3 flashes/sec, below 20% saturation).
- Captions default ON; toggle to OFF available in the player chrome (not part of the recording).

## Demo data reminders for the recording engineer

- Login URLs and credentials are listed in the task brief — do not show the password characters in the recording (blur the password field or use a password manager autofill so characters are masked).
- For SAPE Scene 11 and 12, pre-seed the two products in the POS catalogue: one with a verified GTIN tied to the Tiger Brands → Massmart → Crown → Mama Thandi custody chain; one with an unverified GTIN flagged as counterfeit. If the live SAPE module is not yet wired to a real registry in the build, record the verification panel from the seeded demo state — the 412 ms latency figure should be displayed as a real number from the verification endpoint, not a hardcoded string.
- The "NEW" badge on the SAPE sidebar item should be the amber-warm accent, not emerald — to distinguish "new feature" from "trusted/verified".
