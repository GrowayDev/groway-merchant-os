## 1. Overview of SA Professional Regulatory Framework

South Africa has a **dual regulatory system** for professional services:

1. **Statutory Councils** - Established by Acts of Parliament, mandatory registration
2. **Voluntary Professional Bodies** - Industry associations, optional membership

### Key Regulatory Bodies

| Type | Examples | Mandatory? |
|------|----------|------------|
| Statutory Councils | HPCSA, ECSA, SAICA, LPC | YES - Illegal to practice without |
| SETAs | FASSET, BANKSETA, HWSETA | NO - But governs skills development |
| Voluntary Bodies | SAIPA, IITPSA, SAIM | NO - But provides credibility |

## 2. Statutory Professional Councils

### 2.1 Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA)

**Establishment:** Health Professions Act, 1974 (Act 56 of 1974)
**Mandatory Registration:** YES - Illegal to practice without

**Professional Boards Under HPCSA:**

| Board | Professions Regulated |
|-------|----------------------|
| Medical & Dental | Medical practitioners, dentists |
| Pharmacy | Pharmacists, pharmacy technicians |
| Nursing | Professional nurses, staff nurses, nursing auxiliaries |
| Psychology | Clinical, counseling, educational, research psychologists |
| Physiotherapy | Physiotherapists, physiotherapy assistants |
| Radiography | Radiographers |
| Dietetics | Dietitians, nutritionists |
| Occupational Therapy | Occupational therapists |
| Speech-Language | Speech-language therapists, audiologists |
| Emergency Care | Emergency care practitioners |
| Optometry | Optometrists, dispensing opticians |
| Dental Assisting | Dental assistants, dental technicians |

**Key Admin Functions:**
- Annual registration renewal
- CPD point tracking (minimum 30 CEU per year)
- Practice management registration
- Complaints and disciplinary procedures
- Scope of practice enforcement

### 2.2 Engineering Council of South Africa (ECSA)

**Establishment:** Engineering Profession Act, 2000 (Act 46 of 2000)
**Mandatory Registration:** YES for engineering practitioners

**Registration Categories:**

| Category | Requirements |
|----------|--------------|
| Candidate | Completed engineering qualification |
| Professional Engineer (PrEng) | 4 years experience + professional review |
| Professional Engineering Technologist (PrTechEng) | 3 years experience + professional review |
| Professional Certificated Engineer (PrCertEng) | Government Certificate of Competence |
| Professional Engineering Technician (PrTech) | 3 years experience + professional review |
| Professional Technologist (PrTechniEng) | 3 years experience + professional review |
| Specified Categories | Limited scope registration |

**Key Admin Functions:**
- Registration applications and renewals
- CPD compliance (3-year cycle, minimum 25 points)
- Professional interviews
- Continuing engineering education tracking
- Disciplinary committee proceedings

### 2.3 South African Institute of Chartered Accountants (SAICA)

**Establishment:** Auditing Profession Act, 2005
**Mandatory Registration:** YES for auditors and chartered accountants

**Designations:**

| Designation | Requirements |
|-------------|--------------|
| CA(SA) | SAICA training contract + APC + QE 1&2 |
| AGA(SA) | SAIPA/SAICA training + assessment |
| AT(SA) | Accounting technician qualification |
| RA (Registered Auditor) | CA(SA) + IRBA registration |

**Key Admin Functions:**
- Training contract management
- Assessment registration
- CPD tracking (minimum 120 hours per 3-year cycle)
- Ethics compliance
- Practice monitoring
