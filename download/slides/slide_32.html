Onboarding

Spaza Shop Onboarding Process

1
WEEK 1-2

Identification & Recruitment

  • •Field sales identifies qualifying spazas
  • •Owner registers interest
  • •Eligibility verification against SSSF
  • •Consent and documentation
2
WEEK 3

Device Deployment & Setup

  • •Merchant OS account created
  • •Gopay device delivered & activated
  • •Initial stock catalogue loaded
  • •Payment methods configured
3
WEEK 3-4

Training Kick-off

  • •Digital literacy training (2 sessions)
  • •POS and Gopay device training
  • •First Learning Academy course enrolled
  • •Mentor matched
4
MONTH 2

Go-Live & Monitoring

  • •First live transactions
  • •Weekly check-ins for first month
  • •Inventory loading support
  • •Credit customer setup
5
MONTH 3+

Ongoing Support

  • •Monthly business review
  • •Mentorship sessions continue
  • •Quarterly compliance check
  • •Stakeholder reports generated
Support Channels:
groups Field Sales Team
phone_iphone In-App Support
chat WhatsApp Helpdesk
support_agent Mentor Network
  • Full onboarding cycle takes approximately 6-8 weeks to go-live
  • Four support channels ensure no merchant is left without help
  • Stages 1-3 are intensive; Stage 4-5 transition to sustainable operations
  • Weekly check-ins in Month 2 are critical for adoption
